## Delivery notes

- **Open with the number, not the method**: "0.07 or 0.33, same data." Then explain the two clocks.
- **Do not narrate the models.** The interaction coefficient is one sentence; the figures carry the argument. If asked, the model is: age + round fixed effects, person-clustered SEs, interaction for the difference in slopes.
- **Expected question 1 — measurement equivalence.** Answer: same harmonised item battery, verified against both codebooks, but self-reported difficulty is culturally filtered; that is the main limitation, stated in the report.
- **Expected question 2 — causality.** Answer: we show gradients and associations, not causes. Four candidate mechanisms in the Discussion; education is the one we can partially rule out.
- **Expected question 3 — why not weight?** Answer: weights change the level, not the sign or the ratio (we would expect); it is on the limitations list as the next step.
- **Expected question 4 — why ELSA w9 and not w8/w10?** Because w9's fieldwork window is the closest to CHARLS wave 4; the average mismatch is 0.8 years.
- Rehearse the handoffs at slides 5, 8 and 11 so they do not eat the 12 minutes.
- Keep a backup slide with Table 2 (slopes and CIs) in case someone asks for exact numbers.
